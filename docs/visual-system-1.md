# Research Atlas — Visual System 1.0

Visual System 1.0 moves Research Atlas away from a card-heavy SaaS dashboard and toward an editorial research environment.

## Direction

**Calm · Contextual · Precise**

The product should feel closer to an analytical reading environment than a control panel.

## Color architecture

Color is split into four independent roles:

1. **Interface neutrals** — canvas, surfaces, separators, text.
2. **Context color** — restrained product accent. It is not derived from industry labels.
3. **Data colors** — chart-series roles such as primary, cash, working capital, inventory, reference and forecast.
4. **Semantic colors** — success, warning, danger and pending states.

A single color must not simultaneously mean industry, positive performance, verified evidence and a chart series.

## Composition rules

- Prefer whitespace, typography and separators over containers.
- Metrics live directly on the canvas.
- Charts are editorial sections, not floating dashboard cards.
- Findings and research questions use rows and rhythm rather than repeated shadows.
- Company / Product Map and Industry Modules remain available through progressive disclosure.
- Optional AI is secondary and collapsed by default.
- One screen should foreground one research question or task.

## Shape rules

Rounded rectangles are reserved for controls, grouped interactive surfaces and overlays. They are not the default container for every piece of information.

## Motion

Motion communicates continuity only. Page entry is subtle and all transitions respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Typography

Use the system font stack and a small hierarchy:

- Display
- Title
- Body
- Secondary
- Caption

Avoid relying on all-caps metadata and exaggerated tracking for hierarchy.

## Visual references

The direction combines:

- Apple Music: tonal coherence and contextual color
- Apple Stocks: restrained financial semantics
- Apple.com: editorial rhythm and whitespace
- Apple HIG: materials, motion, accessibility and interaction discipline
